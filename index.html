About

My journey into artificial intelligence began with a fascination with both technology and medicine: the human brain, how it works, and how technology could help us understand and support it.

I initially considered becoming a neurosurgeon. Eventually I realised that what I wanted most was to understand the brain and build technologies that could contribute to healthcare.

That led me toward Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering.

Today I work across AI engineering, machine learning, data, software development, automation and AI deployment. I enjoy taking an idea or a real-world problem and turning it into something practical that people can use.

As Founder & CEO of Axterion, I turn ideas and real-world problems into practical technology solutions.
